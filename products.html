SYSTEM CODE: K-AGNI // STRIKE LOITERING

Kytex "Agni"

Agni is a high-speed loitering munition system, often termed a precision strike or kamikaze drone. Fired via a compact pneumatically boosted tube, it circles above the operational zone to scan, identify, and dive-strike combatant targets with supreme accuracy. Its low visual and acoustic signatures make it nearly impossible to detect before strike vector activation.
